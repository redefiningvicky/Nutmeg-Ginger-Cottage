# Nutmeg–Ginger Design · Portfolio Assets

17 individual high-resolution assets exported from the game’s current vector artwork.

- 12 looping GIFs: 1600 × 1120 pixels, 10 frames per second.
- Four circular profile PNGs: 2048 × 2048 pixels, transparent outside the circle.
- Outside cottage PNG: 3000 × 2000 pixels.

The GIFs are silent rendered demonstrations of clickable actions, with subtle click rings and action captions. They loop automatically; they are not playable game files. Room GIFs begin with the appropriate floor plan, then show room items and furniture actions.

Open index.html to browse the complete set. Individual assets can be used directly in a portfolio. The published game has not been changed.
